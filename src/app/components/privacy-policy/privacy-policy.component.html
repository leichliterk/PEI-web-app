1. Introduction

PEI Data Services ("PEI", "we", "us", or "our") operates an industrial monitoring and data services platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use our web application and related services (collectively, the "Service"). Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the Service.

2. Information We Collect

Account Information

When your organization provisions an account, we collect your name, email address, and organizational role. Authentication is managed through Auth0; we do not store your password.

Usage Data

We automatically collect information about how you interact with the Service, including pages viewed, features used, and session timestamps. This data is used solely to operate and improve the Service.

Operational and Sensor Data

The Service collects telemetry from connected industrial sites, including PLC tag readings, equipment status, and event logs. This data is owned by your organization and processed by PEI only to provide the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provision and maintain your account and authenticate your identity.
  • To transmit, store, and display operational data from your organization's sites.
  • To send push notifications and alerts based on rules you configure.
  • To generate reports and analytics requested through the Service.
  • To diagnose technical issues and maintain the security and performance of the Service.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist in delivering the Service (e.g., cloud hosting, authentication, push notifications), each bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Your Organization: Administrators within your tenant account have access to data associated with your organization's sites and users.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or governmental authority.

5. Data Retention

Account information is retained for as long as your account is active. Operational and sensor data is retained in accordance with your organization's service agreement. Upon account deletion, personal information is removed from active systems within 30 days, subject to any legal retention obligations.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (TLS), token-based authentication, and role-based access controls. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

To request deletion of your login credentials, use our account deletion page. Note that operational data associated with your organization's sites is managed by your organization's administrator.

8. Third-Party Services

The Service uses Auth0 for identity management and Firebase for push notifications. Use of these services is subject to their respective privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the Service after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
